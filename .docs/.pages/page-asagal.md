# Angel's+Space Age Galore

![Galore Overview](https://github.com/JTnadrooi/Project-Galore/blob/main/.media/overview-compact-horizontal.png?raw=true)

**SPACE AGE-LESS VERSION [HERE](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/angels_galore)**

## What To Expect

Expect a complete overhaul of (bio-)chemistry, ore processing and metallurgy as inherited from Angel's mods, with their scope expanded to include Space Age. This is built on a modified version of Angel's Special Vanilla (as established in Angel's Galore). This allows for near-perfect integration into Space Age, but does not include the eight extra metals (which, without Bob's mods, would only be used for crafting buildings).

The inclusion of the entire Galore suite makes this probably _the_ "mod with the most ways to do something." Lots of choices will need to be made, all differing per save, so replay value is major. You could, for example, go full bioprocessing, or maybe have a metal-heavy base with a focus on maximum-efficiency metallurgy. This being a proper overhaul mod, I have been able to make the alternate production paths even more in-depth than I ever could in Vanilla Galore or Space Age Galore.

As for difficulty, it's harder than Krastorio 2 but not nearly as hard as AngelBob or Seablock.

## Notes

-   For a full list of changes made to Pure Angel's to make it a smoother playing experience, see the Angel's Galore mod page.
-   **Recipes can be enabled or disabled in the mod settings. Make sure to review these settings before starting your game.**
-   Unlike most large overhaul mods, ASAGAL's lack of Bob's mods has allowed me to make it very stable and compatible with other mods. Play however you like. ## Recommended Mods

-   [Angel's Infinite Ores](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/angelsinfiniteores) by [Arch666Angel](https://mods.factorio.com/user/Arch666Angel); adds tiny infinite ore patches in the center of non-starter ore patches.
-   [Angel's Addons - Robot Logistics](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/angelsaddons-bots) by [Arch666Angel](https://mods.factorio.com/user/Arch666Angel); adds new early bots and roboport variants. Also contains logistic/construction extenders. Content may overlap with the Bob's Logistics mod. (Zone extenders and early robots)
-   [Angel's Addons - Storage Options](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/angelsaddons-storage) by [Arch666Angel](https://mods.factorio.com/user/Arch666Angel); adds larger chests.
-   [Angel's Addons - C.A.B.](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/angelsaddons-cab) by [Arch666Angel](https://mods.factorio.com/user/Arch666Angel); adds a mobile base-type vehicle.
-   [Angel's Addons - Mass Transit](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/angelsaddons-mobility) by [Arch666Angel](https://mods.factorio.com/user/Arch666Angel); adds new higher-tier train types themed for petrochem or smelting. Content may overlap with the Bob's Logistics mod. (Higher-tier trains)
-   [Proper Tier Doubling](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/proper_tier_doubling_port) ported by [JTnadrooi (Me)](https://mods.factorio.com/user/JTnadrooi); adds proper tier doubling for vanilla buildings and belts.
-   Quality by Wube.
